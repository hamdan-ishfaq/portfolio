# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Senior SAP consultants, Big-4 recruiters, and enterprise IT directors evaluating Muhammad Hamdan Ishfaq for SAP S/4HANA MM, supply-chain analytics, and applied automation roles. They scan for ERP credibility first, then proof of delivery.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio converts live SAP internship experience, supply-chain coursework, and production engineering projects into a trustworthy hiring surface. Success means a visitor understands the specialty in one viewport, trusts the operational depth within one scroll, and can reach CV, LinkedIn, email, or case studies without friction.

## Brand Personality

Precise, enterprise-trusted, auditable.

The brand should feel like modern B2B / SAP Fiori-adjacent software: deep navy, slate, crisp white, structured density, and purposeful interaction. It should not feel like a neon AI-lab demo, a template portfolio, or a dashboard wearing decorative gradients.

## Anti-references

- Generic purple-on-white SaaS landing pages
- Neon / copper “AI lab” aesthetics
- Inflated metrics or invented credentials
- Decorative 3D that does not map to a real process

## Strategic Principles

1. Honesty over polish — metrics and credentials are verbatim and verifiable.
2. Process literacy over buzzwords — P2P/O2C and document flow beat vague AI claims.
3. One job per section — Experience proves tenure; Certifications prove credentials; Projects prove build quality.
4. Interaction serves understanding — the depot simulator teaches document posting; motion never distracts from scanability.
